# Source attribution

CARE-VLA uses RoboCasa and RoboCasa365 simulated scenes and assets, adapted by adding a synthetic articulated actor, task fixtures, inert markers, and replay cameras. Cite Nasiriany et al., RoboCasa365 (ICLR 2026) and RoboCasa (RSS 2024). RoboCasa upstream lists its code under MIT and its assets and datasets under CC BY 4.0.

- [RoboCasa source and licensing](https://github.com/robocasa/robocasa#license)
- [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
- [RoboCasa365 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.04356)

Model weights and upstream asset archives are obtained from their original sources under those sources' terms. Checkpoint revisions and the inference environment are documented in the model results. This file records attribution for review and the provenance of the generated media.
